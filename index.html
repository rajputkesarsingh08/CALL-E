FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Does CampusConnect make a real phone call?

Yes. The production architecture sends the task to CALL-E through the backend. The application should never claim that a call happened when CALL-E is not configured.

Can I control what the AI asks?

Yes. Additional instructions and preferred outcomes can be included in the call planning request.

What information is stored?

The backend can store call metadata, status, structured result and transcript when available.
